Scam sites ask your wallet for things you would never agree to if you could read them. ThriveeShield sits between the site and your NEAR wallet, reads the request, and stops the dangerous ones before your wallet is even asked.

What the shield did
  • Press a button on the fake site. Each request is checked and the result appears here.

Three outcomes

ALLOWEDNothing unusualThe request goes straight to your wallet. You only see a small "checked" note.
WARNINGSomething to look atA new receiver, a permission over your assets, a large amount. You decide whether to continue.
BLOCKEDA known attackFull-access key requests, account deletion, code installs, blocklisted addresses. Your wallet is never asked.

Use the real extension

  1. Download the code from github.com/AffanAR/thriveeshield (Code, then Download ZIP) and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Press Load unpacked and choose the extension folder.

It is built to guard Meteor, HOT, Intear, Nightly, Sender, Bitget Wallet and OKX Wallet on NEAR. It is not in the Chrome Web Store, and it is a hackathon prototype that has not been audited.

Built for NEAR HACKATHON, track AI Security & Defense. ThriveeShield never holds your keys and never signs anything.
